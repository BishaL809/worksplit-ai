💡 How to Use WorkSplit (Easy Guide)

WorkSplit AI helps your group track shared work money and expenses so everyone knows exactly who owes whom—with zero arguments.

1️⃣

Select or Create a Group

Go to Groups to create a team (e.g. "Ahmedabad Work", "College Project", or "Roommates") and add your friends' names.

2️⃣

Add Money In & Money Out

💰 Income: When someone receives payment for the group's work, record it under Income so everyone gets their fair share.

🛒 Expenses: When a member buys food, travel, or tools, add an Expense. Our smart AI will pick the category automatically!

3️⃣

Check "Settle Up"

Click Settle Up to see who pays whom. The app finds the fewest payments so all debts are cleared quickly.

Understanding Your Balance:
  • 🟢 Green (+): You get money back! Other members will pay you.
  • 🔴 Red (-): You owe money. You need to pay another member.
  • ⚪ Settled (₹0.00): You are completely clear!
