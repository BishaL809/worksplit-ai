💡 How the Math Works (Simple & 100% Accurate)

No guessing or AI in financial calculations. The math is simple, fair, and transparent:

Your Net Balance = (Work Earned − Cash Received In Hand) + (Paid Out of Pocket − Your Expense Share) + (Settlements Paid − Settlements Received)

• Positive (+₹): You contributed more than you received → You will get money back.
• Negative (−₹): You received money or owe your share → You need to pay.
• Balanced (₹0.00): Total money to pay always equals total money to receive.
